About Football

Football is the world's most popular sport, played by more than 250 million people in over 200 countries. Two teams of eleven players try to score by getting the ball into the opponent's goal, without using their hands.

From local pitches to packed stadiums, the game brings communities together. GoalZone helps you follow your favourite clubs, learn about the leagues and never miss a match.
